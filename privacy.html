DuDu Publisher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information DuDu Publisher may access when you connect it to a TikTok account, and how that information is used.

DuDu Publisher is a desktop publishing tool operated for the Learn Chinese with DuDu project. It is a self-use tool: the account you authorize is normally your own.

This website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts.

Information that may be accessed through TikTok OAuth

When you authorize DuDu Publisher through the TikTok Login Kit, the tool may receive:

  • Basic profile information
  • open_id
  • Display name
  • Authorization tokens (access token and refresh token, where issued)

Depending on the scopes you approve, the tool may also read basic account profile fields needed to confirm which account is connected.

How the information is used

  • To authorize and maintain the connection to your TikTok account.
  • To upload and publish a video when you explicitly request it in the application.
  • To verify the connected account and to show its display name so you can confirm you are publishing to the right account.

Information obtained through TikTok is not used for any purpose other than account authorization and the upload or publishing action you initiate.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising profiles, ad targeting, or audience building.
  • We do not share TikTok data with third-party marketers.
  • We do not use the tool for bulk posting, spam, or unauthorized account operation.
  • We do not collect passwords, and the tool never asks for your TikTok password.

Authorization tokens

Authorization tokens are used only to make the authorized TikTok API calls on your behalf — reading basic profile information you have approved, and uploading or publishing a video when you request it.

Tokens are stored locally on the device running the application and are treated as credentials: they are not published, not embedded in this website, and not shared.

You can revoke DuDu Publisher’s access at any time from your TikTok account settings. Revoking access there removes the application’s authorization, after which the tool can no longer call the TikTok API for that account.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures appropriate to a small self-use tool: authorization tokens are held locally rather than transmitted anywhere else, credentials are excluded from version control, and the tool does not log or display secret values.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Retention

Information obtained through TikTok OAuth is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can end retention of the authorization by revoking access through TikTok, and by removing the local application data from your device.

Children

DuDu Publisher is a content publishing tool for the creator of Learn Chinese with DuDu. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

DuDu Publisher is operated by the creator of the Learn Chinese with DuDu project. This project is an independent work and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support contact published on the project’s official channels.

This policy deliberately does not state a postal address, telephone number, or legal entity, because none is registered for this project.
